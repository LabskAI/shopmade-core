# Design direction

- Calm and legible: a warm off-white ground, dark ink, one green accent for the call button and links.
- Mobile first. The phone number is the main action and stays in the header on every page.
- Change the look through `theme/tokens.css` only (colors, type scale, spacing, radius). Layout, navigation, footer and structured data belong to the front kit.
- Body text at 17px or more, lines under 70 characters, color contrast at WCAG AA or better.
- Square corners (`--radius: 0`) unless the owner asks for something else.
- No stock photos. Use real photos only when the owner provides them.
