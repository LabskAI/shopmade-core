# Brand voice

Write like the owner of a small, careful cleaning crew talking to a neighbour.

- Short sentences and concrete nouns: kitchens, baseboards, move-out walkthroughs. No hype words.
- Say what the customer gets and when, not how good we are.
- One idea per paragraph. Use a list for anything with three or more items.
- Headlines name the service and the place: "Office cleaning in Oak Hollow", not "Excellence in every corner".
- Spanish pages use "usted" and are written in Spanish, not translated word for word. Keep the same facts and structure as the English page.
- Copy the phone number, cities and service names exactly as they appear in facts.yaml.
