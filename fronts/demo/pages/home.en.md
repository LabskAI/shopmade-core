# Clean homes and offices in Naples, Estero and Bonita Springs

Demo Home Services is a small cleaning crew for houses, offices and move-outs. We work in English and Spanish, and the same people come back every visit.

## What we clean

- **Houses** — weekly, every other week, or once before guests arrive.
- **Offices** — after hours, so your team never works around a vacuum.
- **Move-outs** — empty homes made ready for the next family or the final walkthrough.

## How booking works

1. Call us or send an email with the address and what needs cleaning.
2. We agree on a day and a time that suit you.
3. Recurring visits keep the same crew and the same checklist.
